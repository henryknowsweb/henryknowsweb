about Henry

Henry — marketplace operator & Shopify designer, working worldwide.

I came into this through e-commerce. For more than six years I worked with online stores and marketplaces: Amazon, eBay, TikTok Shop, Walmart, Temu and Mercari, plus Shopify stores. My first job was at Tradecozone, before I started my degree, and I kept working in e-commerce through my studies.

The work covers listing images and graphics, A+ content, PPC, reconciliation and fixing accounts that have problems. I design in Figma, run email with Klaviyo and handle support with Gorgias and Miracle Connect.

In 2026 I completed a Bachelor’s in Computer Science, majoring in web and mobile application development. My final year project won first prize in the graduation project program, and I won first prize in the mock interview program. Because I can build, I also make software for businesses, like Sellomize Reach, as a side project.

I work remotely with clients worldwide. Message me on TikTok (@henryknowsweb) or by email, tell me which marketplaces you sell on and what isn’t working, and we’ll start from there.
