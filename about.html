about Henry

Henry — marketplace operator & Shopify designer, working worldwide.

I came into web design through e-commerce. For more than six years I worked with online stores on Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Shopify and Temu. My first job was at Tradecozone, before I had even started my degree, and I kept working in e-commerce all the way through my studies. Those years taught me how selling online actually works — what a product page has to show, where a checkout loses people, and why a store that is hard to buy from stays quiet.

In 2026 I completed my Bachelor’s in Computer Science, majoring in web and mobile application development. My final year project took first prize in the graduation project program, and I also won first prize in the mock interview program. While studying, I built apps and websites of my own and kept taking on e-commerce work wherever it came.

Now I build the kind of stores I used to work in. I know what a marketplace listing has to do to sell because I have done that job, and I know how to build the site because that is what I trained for. If your products sell on a marketplace but your own website does not, that gap is exactly what I fix.

I work remotely with clients worldwide, and everything runs through my social channels — that’s where I post my builds, and where every project starts. Message me there or by email, tell me what you sell and what isn’t working on your current site, and you’ll see a first version to react to before I build the full site. Then we launch together.
